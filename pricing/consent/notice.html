Cookies? Your call. 🍪

Your car gets a price either way. Optional analytics help us understand visits and which pricing tools people use.

If you accept, we use a random visitor cookie (90 days) and a session ID to link visits with vehicle characteristics, Ask/Bid estimates, valuation dates and UTC timestamps. We collect broad browser/device categories and approximate country, region and city when available. VPN/mobile locations may be wrong.

No fingerprinting, precise location, raw IP addresses or seller descriptions are stored in our analytics. Turso hosts the private records. Records are retained for 90 days; cleanup runs during consented visits and via a daily job when configured. Hosting providers may maintain their own operational logs.

The necessary preference cookie remembers your choice for 90 days. You can change it here anytime. Withdrawal stops future collection; contact the operator for deletion of earlier records, quoting your visitor ID below.

Location sources: GeoJS privacy / MaxMind GeoLite. For optional local databases: IP Geolocation by DB-IP or MaxMind GeoLite2.
