# Privacy Policy

*Updated: 8 November 2026*

## First, the simple version

This is a free tool for used-car prices.

- You can use it without an account.
- You do not have to say yes to tracking.
- We use car facts to work out a price.
- Please do not share your name or home address in a car story.
- You can ask us to delete data we have kept.

**Tracking** means saving facts about how you use this site.
We ask before we turn on extra tracking.

## Who runs this site?

My name is **Amit Steinberg**.
I run this site as a private person in **Hamburg, Germany**.
You can email me at **amitsteinberg74@gmail.com**.

I choose how this site uses personal data.
The law calls me the **data controller**.

## What happens when you price a car?

You give us facts like the car's age, make, and miles.
You may also add a story, a car ad, or a photo.
The tool reads this to give you a price guess.

You do not need to give us your real name.
Do not add your phone number or address to a car story.

We use these facts to run the free tool.
The law lets us do this to serve our site.
This is called a **legitimate interest**.
See GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

We do not use the price guess to make choices about your job, loans, or legal rights.

Our web host is **Streamlit**.
It needs some data, such as your IP address, to send you the site.
An **IP address** is a number used by your device online.
The host may also keep logs to keep the site safe.
We do not control how long the host keeps these logs.
We rely on legitimate interest to run and protect the site.

## Can I say no to tracking?

**Yes.** Open **Privacy & cookies**.
Choose **Reject analytics**.
The price tool will still work.

We save your yes or no choice in a small file called a **cookie**.
This cookie can stay for up to **90 days**.

We do not start the extra tools below unless you say yes.

## What happens if I say yes?

Three tools may use data about your visit:

**1. Turso helps us fix bugs.**

Turso is a place where we store site data.
We give your browser a random code.
It is not your name.
We may save car facts, price results, dates, and pages used.
We may also save broad device facts, error codes, and a rough city.

Our Turso tracking tables do **not** save full car stories.
They do not save raw IP numbers or GPS points.
**GPS** means a device's exact location.

**2. GeoJS makes a rough location guess.**

Your browser may ask **GeoJS** for your city and country.
GeoJS sees your IP address during this check.
Our site saves only the rough place, not the IP.
The guess may be wrong.
You can fix the city in the privacy box.

**3. Google Analytics counts site use.**

Google can get your IP, browser facts, and cookies.
It can see which pages and tools you use.
We do not send your full car story to Google.
We do not send car facts in shared links to Google.
We turn off Google ad features.

Your yes is called **consent**.
It means you gave us leave to use these extra tools.
Our legal rule is GDPR Article 6(1)(a).
German cookie law also applies (TDDDG, Section 25).

## How long do you keep data?

Our choice cookie can last **90 days**.
We also set Google cookies to last up to **90 days**.

We aim to clear Turso visit data after **90 days**.
The clean-up may run late.
Google has its own rules and time limits.
We have not yet checked the Google account's full data time limit.

## Can I change my mind?

**Yes, at any time.**
Open **Privacy & cookies**.
Choose **Reject analytics**.
This stops new extra tracking and clears its browser code.

Old records do not vanish at once.
You can ask me to remove them.
Email **amitsteinberg74@gmail.com**.

If you have a visitor code, please send it.
It helps me find your records.

## What if I send a sale report?

You can choose to send us a car's real sale price.
You may add a photo as proof.
We save this only when you send it.
This is a **separate choice** from tracking.
Our legal rule for the report is consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

We may save the car facts, sale price, date, and proof.
We do not put your report on a public page.
We do not use it to train models by default.
You get a secret code that lets you delete your report.
Keep that code safe.

We aim to clear sale reports after **90 days**.
That clean-up may run late.

## What can I ask you to do?

The law is called the **GDPR**.

You can ask me to:
- Tell you what data I have about you.
- Fix data that is wrong.
- Delete data when the law gives you that right.
- Stop or limit some uses of your data.
- Give you a copy of some data.
- Stop using data in some cases.
- Stop tracking after you gave consent.

Email **amitsteinberg74@gmail.com**.
I will answer as the law says.
I may need your visitor code to find your records.

You can also make a complaint to a data protection office.
In Hamburg, you can contact the
[Hamburg data protection office](https://datenschutz-hamburg.de/service-information/beschwerde-oder-hinweis-einreichen).
You can also contact another EU office if the law lets you.

Ask them before you send a sale report too.
