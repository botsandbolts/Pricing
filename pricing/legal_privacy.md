# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

## The short version

Car Price Studio is a free tool to study used-car prices. You do not need an account. There are no ads.

You can turn down optional tracking and still use the price tools. We use the car details you enter to work out a price. Please do not put your name, phone number, home address, or other private details in a car description.

This page tells you what data we use, why we use it, and how you can make a choice.

Some words you may see here: **GDPR** is the EU law about personal data. **Consent** means that you said yes. An **IP address** is a number used when your device goes online.

## When you use the site

The tools need the car facts you enter, such as make, model, year, miles, and condition. Some tools also read your car description, a listing, or an image you upload. We use this data to give you the result you ask for. The price is an estimate, not a real offer.

We process this data to run the free tool. Our legal basis is a *legitimate interest* in providing the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use the estimate to make legal or credit decisions about you.

The site runs on Streamlit hosting. Like most web hosts, it may handle your IP address and basic connection logs to send the site to your device and keep it safe. This is also based on a legitimate interest in running and securing the site. The host sets its own log rules and time limits, which are outside my own Turso clean-up.

## Your choice about tracking

A small file called a **cookie** can help a site recall a choice. We use a choice cookie for up to **90 days**. It tells us if you accepted or rejected optional tracking.

If you **reject** tracking, the car calculator still works. We do not start our optional Turso tracking, Google Analytics, or GeoJS location check.

If you **accept**, we may use:

- **Turso:** A random visitor ID and session ID. We keep car facts, price results, dates, broad device details, page and feature actions, basic error codes, and a rough location. We do **not** save your full car description, raw IP address, or GPS data in our Turso tracking tables.
- **GeoJS:** Your browser may ask GeoJS to guess your city and country from your IP address. GeoJS receives your IP when this happens. We keep only the rough location, not the IP. The guess can be wrong. You can correct the city.
- **Google Analytics:** Google may receive your IP and browser details, its own cookies, and page and feature-use events. We do not send car descriptions, your Turso visitor ID, or the car details in shared links to Google. We turn off ad features.

The legal basis for this optional tracking is **your consent** (GDPR Article 6(1)(a), and Section 25 of Germany's TDDDG where it applies).

Google Analytics cookies are set for up to **90 days** by our current code. Our Turso records are set for a **90-day clean-up**. Clean-up jobs may be late, so a record might stay longer. Google's own data storage rules and the account's retention setting are separate; the exact account setting has not yet been verified.

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. Open **Privacy & cookies** on the site, then choose **Reject analytics**. This stops new optional tracking and clears the related visitor cookie in your browser. It does not undo data already used with your past consent.

You can ask me to delete older data at **amitsteinberg74@gmail.com**. If you have the random visitor ID shown in the privacy tool, send it with your request. It helps me find the right records without asking who you are.

## If you send a sale report

Some tools let you send a car sale price or proof, such as a receipt photo, for private review. This is **separate** from site tracking. We only save it after you choose to send it. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

We save the car details, sale facts, and any proof you choose to share. We do not add this report to public pages or use it to train a model by default. You get a private deletion key. Keep it safe: you can use it to delete your report. We aim to clear reports after 90 days, but clean-up may run late.

If you email me, I use your email and message to reply to your request. My legal basis is a legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). I do not use it to send ads. I keep the message while I handle your request, and longer only if the law calls for it.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask what personal data I hold about you. You may ask me to fix, delete, or limit its use. You may also object to some uses or ask for a copy you can move to another service, where the law gives you that right.

You can withdraw consent at any time. Email **amitsteinberg74@gmail.com** to ask for help. I will respond as the law requires. If the data has no name tied to it, I may need your visitor ID or deletion key to find it.

You can also complain to a data protection office. In Hamburg, this is the [Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://datenschutz-hamburg.de/service-information/beschwerde-oder-hinweis-einreichen). You may also contact another EU data protection authority where the law allows.
